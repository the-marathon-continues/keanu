## The Living Ratio

σ = convergence / divergence

```
σ > 1: More coming together. Growth. Focus.
σ = 1: Equilibrium. Balance.
σ < 1: More spreading out. Exploration. Space.
```

Healthy systems oscillate. Pure convergence is tunnel vision. Pure divergence is chaos.

## The Point

Layer 0 is the physics that everything else rests on. Four dimensions:

- Convergence and divergence in space (yin/yang)
- Throughline in linear time (past→future)
- Loop in cyclical time (return/spiral)

Together they define the substrate. Is it coming together or spreading out? Is time flowing or stuck? Is the cycle turning or frozen?

Everything above this layer is psychology built on physics.
